# Niche Matrix — how each archetype reshapes the pack

This is the core adaptation logic. Read it after intake, once you've classified the client. It tells you how each of the six files changes for each archetype, what to add, what to drop, and where the conversion lever sits.

## How to classify

Ask one question: **what is the main thing standing between this business and a sale?**

- If it's *can I trust this and is it allowed* → Regulated / sensitive (A).
- If it's *does this look good enough to want* → Visual / lifestyle / consumer (B).
- If it's *is this the right choice and can they deliver* → Considered / B2B / service (C).

Secondary signal: regulation. If a regulator, licensing body, or platform ad policy restricts what the business may claim, it's A regardless of how pretty the product is.

## Archetype A — Regulated / sensitive

Niches: health, wellness, pharma-adjacent, supplements, cosmeceuticals, finance, insurance, investing, legal, medical aesthetics, cannabis, gambling, anything making health/money/legal claims.

The conversion problem is almost never lead volume. It's the trust gap and the claims ceiling. People want to buy but ask "who's behind this, is it allowed, does it actually work, will it embarrass me." Every creative decision is measured against whether it builds credibility and stays inside the line.

- **00 read-me** — State plainly that file 03 (language) is the most important file and must be consulted before any public-facing content. The Project Instructions block should hard-wire "read the language file before producing any public content."
- **01 overview** — Lead with the credible human: the founder's story, the named expert or advisor, their real credentials and years of experience. This is the trust anchor the whole account leans on.
- **02 offerings** — Frame categories as indications or use-cases, not product hype. For each: what it addresses, the genuine selling point, the audience, the positioning against the status quo (expensive consult, awkward process, grey market). Keep every claim defensible.
- **03 language** — Mandatory and detailed. Build a real **approved language** list (the exact compliant phrasings) and a real **forbidden language** list (claims, words, and ingredient names that cannot appear in promotion). Add short campaign lines that stay inside the line and a single closing slogan. State the governing principle: viral or disruptive work operates through **association and humor, never direct mention** of the condition, ingredients, or treatment.
- **04 workstreams** — Trust-building work comes first (expert videos, founder story, credibility content), then the disruptive/viral layer that carries that offline trust online. Each workstream carries its compliance ground rules inline.
- **05 principles** — The headline learning: the trust gap is the central conversion metric, not lead volume. "Expert as hero" beats "product as hero." Name the strategic reference brand if the operator uses one.

## Archetype B — Visual / lifestyle / consumer

Niches: restaurants and food, fashion and apparel, retail and ecommerce, beauty and cosmetics, travel and hospitality, events, cafes, fitness studios, lifestyle products.

The conversion problem is desire and discovery. The product mostly sells itself if it looks good and reaches the right feed. Compliance is minimal; the risk is blandness and inconsistency, not regulators.

- **00 read-me** — Standard orientation. No heavy compliance flag.
- **01 overview** — Foreground the brand world, the vibe, and the audience persona. Stakeholders matter less than the aesthetic and the customer.
- **02 offerings** — Becomes product lines, menus, or collections. Describe sensory and emotional appeal, signature items, price tiers, what's hero vs supporting.
- **03 language** — Collapses into a short **brand voice** section: tone, words the brand loves, words the brand avoids, emoji and punctuation conventions, the campaign tagline. No legal lists. Title it as voice and style, not compliance.
- **04 workstreams** — Visual-led work leads: shoots, UGC, seasonal campaigns, launches, influencer collabs, social proof. Each with format and cadence.
- **05 principles** — Aesthetic consistency, reach, and social proof are the metrics. Keep the technical spec focused on visual output (image specs, formats, locale/RTL handling if relevant).

## Archetype C — Considered / B2B / service

Niches: real estate, SaaS and tech, professional services (legal, accounting, consulting, agencies), education and courses, automotive, home services, high-ticket B2C services.

The conversion problem is confidence in a considered decision. Buyers research, compare, and need to believe the provider is the right choice and can deliver. Funnels are longer; authority and proof do the work.

- **00 read-me** — Flag that authority and proof content is the spine of the account.
- **01 overview** — Lead with track record, named expertise, and credibility markers (years, clients served, notable wins, certifications).
- **02 offerings** — Becomes solutions or packages. For each: the problem it solves, the outcome, the proof, and where it sits on price/effort. Outcomes and ROI over features.
- **03 language** — Medium weight: claim hygiene and proof discipline (don't overpromise, substantiate numbers, required disclaimers if any), plus positioning language and the value proposition. Lighter than A, heavier than B.
- **04 workstreams** — Case studies, lead nurture, educational content, authority-building, and longer-form assets lead. Each with its funnel stage.
- **05 principles** — Authority and pipeline are the metrics. "Founder/expert as hero" applies here too. Note the longer measurement window.

## Blended clients

Many real clients straddle two archetypes (a medical aesthetics clinic is A+B; a fintech SaaS is A+C; a premium restaurant group is B+C). Rules:

- For **compliance**, always take the stricter archetype. If any part is regulated, file 03 follows A.
- For **creative and offerings framing**, blend: borrow the visual richness of B or the proof discipline of C as the client's actual mix demands.
- Note the blend explicitly in file 05 so the downstream project understands the dual nature.
