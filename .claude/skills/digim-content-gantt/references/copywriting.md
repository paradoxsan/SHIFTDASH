# Copywriting — Short & Long Captions

Every feed post in the gantt carries **two caption options** so the client can pick: a **short** one
and a **long** one. Both in the **brand's voice** (from `references/research_voice_and_creative.md`)
and the **locale's language** (from `references/timing_and_locale.md`). Stories carry their own
short overlay copy. This is process step 7, after the sign-off gate.

---

## The two options per post

**Short caption** — one to two lines. A scroll-stopper: a hook or a single sharp idea + a light
CTA. This is for when the visual carries the message and the caption just nudges. Match the brand's
emoji habit (none if they use none).

**Long caption** — a few short paragraphs. Room for story, value, or detail: hook → substance →
CTA, with hashtags at the end. Still tight and human — long does not mean padded. Use line breaks
the way the brand does.

Write them as genuine alternatives with different angles, not the same sentence stretched. The
client should be choosing between two real options.

---

## Voice & language rules

- **Reproduce the voice profile.** Tone, POV, sentence rhythm, emoji set, hashtag pattern, CTA
  style, signature phrases — all as observed. A clinic stays composed; a gym brand brings 🔥.
- **Write natively, never translate.** Hebrew for Israeli brands; German/Italian/etc. for those
  audiences — composed fresh in that language with the right register (du/Sie, tu/Lei). Translated
  copy reads wrong; don't do it.
- **Hebrew copy — sound like a person, not an AI.** No robotic connectors ("יתרה מזאת", "לסיכום"),
  no generic filler, no over-formal stiffness unless that's the brand. Short, natural, spoken-feel
  lines. Real CTAs ("שלח/י לנו הודעה", "קבעו תור", "לינק בביו"), not "אל תהססו לפנות".
- **CTA fits the goal.** Awareness → save/share/tag; leads → DM/form/link; sales → offer + urgency;
  foot traffic → location/hours/come-in.

---

## Descriptions (תיאור הפוסט)

Separate from the captions, each post needs a **description**: what the post *is* — the concept and
what's on screen — so the client can produce it. One or two sentences. Examples:
- "ריל אנרגטי, חיתוכים מהירים על ביט, מראה את הלקוחות מתאמנים בבוקר."
- "קרוסלה: שקופית לכל תרגיל, טקסט גדול על הסרטון, רקע נקי."

The description is direction for the shoot; the captions are what gets posted with it.

---

## Per-format copy shape

- **ריל (Reel)** — hook in the first line (it doubles as the on-screen hook). Caption supports, CTA
  to follow/save/tag.
- **פוסט (single image)** — the caption usually carries more weight; lead with the point.
- **קרוסלה (Carousel)** — "save this" energy; caption sets up the value and tells them to swipe.
- **סטורי (Story)** — see below.

---

## Stories copy

Stories get **short overlay/sticker text**, not full captions — what's typed on the story itself.
Keep it to a few words, casual, and **interactive** where it fits: a poll ("איזה אימון בא לכם?"),
a question sticker ("שאלו אותנו הכל"), a countdown, a quiz, or a swipe-up/link nudge. One clear
next action per story. Stories live in their own section of the gantt.

---

## Hashtags

Match the brand's **observed** pattern — the same count, the same blend of branded + niche +
local-language tags. Don't bolt on 30 generic tags if they use five. Put them at the end of the
long caption (`hashtags` field). If the brand uses none, use none.

---

## Honesty

Write only what the client can actually stand behind: no invented testimonials, no "מאות לקוחות
מרוצים" unless it's true, no fake urgency. Build the copy to the asset that will exist for that
slot (real footage, or a Drive file) — see `references/drive_media.md`.
